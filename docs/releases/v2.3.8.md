## Summary

**2.3.8** ships **Settings S6 — Advanced / Risk mode** and a round of **hotfixes / improvements** to limits, estimation, notices, and the desktop overlay scrollbar.

## Features

- **Risk mode (S6)** — opt-in in Settings → Advanced / Risk; bypasses Camaleon pixel, byte, and consent limits (500/250 MB hard cap); Wasm `set_risk_mode` on all crates; workspace banner; SVG security unchanged.

## Hotfixes & improvements

- **Limit pipeline** — Wasm applies Risk before session ceiling; estimate/transmute stay in sync; hard-file and pixel blockers when Risk is toggled off mid-session.
- **Estimation** — no redundant re-estimate on tab refocus when fingerprint unchanged.
- **Notices** — deduplicated Risk banners; stronger **Minimal** notice density.
- **Overlay scrollbar** — instant scroll during drag (`scroll-behavior: smooth` bypass); imperative thumb paint; native grab-point drag.
